# Student Research Ideas

This starter document can be replaced with detailed project descriptions.

## Additive Manufacturing Monitoring
- Multi-sensor monitoring for fused-filament fabrication and continuous fiber printing.
- Feature extraction from optical, thermal, and mechanical signals.
- Real-time quality assessment and defect classification.

## Smart Sensing
- Flexible capacitive sensors on concrete and composite structures.
- Signal conditioning and sensor packaging for harsh environments.
- Calibration procedures for non-uniform surfaces.

## Digital Twins and High-Rate ML
- Reduced-order model updating for structural systems.
- High-rate anomaly detection using streaming data.
- Edge computing for sub-millisecond decision support.

## Battery Safety
- Embedded sensing for impact and capacity loss monitoring.
- Early failure indicators in lithium-ion cells.
- Battery degradation modeling with experimental data.
