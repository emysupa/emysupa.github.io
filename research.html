Adaptive Autonomy

We design algorithms that allow autonomous systems to update their behavior while operating in uncertain, changing, or resource-limited environments.

Representative questions

  • How can systems recognize when operating conditions have changed?
  • How can models be updated without compromising safety?
  • How should real-time decisions incorporate uncertainty?

Example projects

  • Online model adaptation for autonomous vehicles.
  • Safety-aware reinforcement learning for mission planning.
  • Decision-making under limited sensing and computation.

Structural Health Monitoring

Our group studies high-rate sensing, damage detection, feature extraction, and diagnostics for systems exposed to dynamic loading.

Methods

  • Signal processing and feature fusion.
  • Physics-guided machine learning.
  • Sensor placement and uncertainty quantification.

Applications

  • Aerospace structures.
  • Mechanical systems.
  • Civil infrastructure.

Digital Twins and Prognostics

We build computational twins that combine sensor data, physics models, and probabilistic forecasts to estimate system state and remaining useful life.

  • Hybrid modeling that integrates measured data with simulations.
  • Real-time prognostics for components and systems.
  • Uncertainty-aware prediction and verification.

Resilient Infrastructure

We develop approaches for monitoring and decision support in infrastructure systems affected by aging, extreme events, and operational uncertainty.

  • Event detection and condition assessment.
  • Risk-informed maintenance and inspection planning.
  • Community resilience and system-level modeling.

Aerospace Systems

Research in this area focuses on flight systems, mission-critical structures, and high-rate dynamic events where sensing and decisions must occur quickly.

  • Mission-aware prognostics and health management.
  • High-rate structural response characterization.
  • Autonomous inspection and adaptive control.
