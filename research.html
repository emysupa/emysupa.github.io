Research

Research Areas

The research program integrates experiments, modeling, sensing, and computation for intelligent engineered systems. The central goal is to validate, update, and control physical systems quickly enough for real-time decisions.

In-Situ Manufacturing Monitoring Smart Materials and Innovative Sensing Digital Twins and Smart Manufacturing High-Rate Machine Learning Battery Safety and Management Systems

In-Situ Manufacturing Monitoring

In-situ monitoring and online validation of additively manufactured components using optical, thermal, mechanical, and embedded sensing systems. The work focuses on real-time quality validation during fabrication for fused-filament fabrication, metal additive manufacturing, and continuous fiber printing.

  • Real-time process validation during additive manufacturing.
  • Multi-sensor data fusion for quality control.
  • Geometric drift tracking and thermo-mechanical modeling.

Related work: multi-sensor data fusion review, simulation-in-the-loop additive manufacturing, and thin-wall FFF digital twin monitoring.

Smart Materials and Innovative Sensing

This area develops smart materials and sensing technologies for structural health monitoring. Contributions include large-area capacitive sensors, nanocomposite sensors, soft elastomeric capacitors, sensing skins, and embedded flexible electronics for concrete, composites, and smart structures.

  • Flexible capacitive sensors for non-uniform surfaces and concrete structures.
  • Adhesive-free bonding of large-area sensors to concrete.
  • Signal conditioning and sensor architecture redesign to reduce capacitive coupling.

Related work: adhesive-free sensing skins, electrically isolated capacitive sensing skins, and soft elastomeric capacitors for concrete strain sensing.

Digital Twins and Smart Manufacturing

The digital twin thrust integrates robotics, automation, control, and cyber-physical synchronization for intelligent production. Current directions include AI-enhanced process optimization, secure digital manufacturing, and real-time structural validation.

  • Reduced-order and local eigenvalue modification procedures for fast model updating.
  • Simulation-in-the-loop methods for structural validation.
  • Robotics and automation for manufacturing research and education.

Related work: O(n) digital twin updating, real-time structural model updating, and Ph.D. dissertation.

High-Rate Machine Learning

This area combines advanced structural modeling with high-rate machine learning for dynamic environments. The focus is on rapid data processing, anomaly detection, high-speed model assimilation, and sub-millisecond decision support.

  • Real-time state estimation and damage detection.
  • High-rate structural twinning and optimal sampling.
  • Edge-oriented algorithms for time-critical systems.

Related work: online model-based structural damage detection, high-rate structural twinning, and real-time solver for local eigenvalue modification.

Battery Safety and Management Systems

This research explores monitoring and control approaches that improve battery safety, reliability, longevity, and performance. Applications include electric vehicles, renewable energy storage, portable electronics, and structural battery systems.

  • Impact-induced capacity loss monitoring in structural batteries.
  • Failure detection in lithium-ion cells under high-C discharge.
  • Embedded sensing for multifunctional energy-storage structures.

Related work: structural batteries with flexible electronics, impact monitoring of embedded batteries, and early detection of 18650 cell failure.
