Project Themes

Continuous Fiber Printing

Real-time monitoring, structural validation, optical/thermal/mechanical sensing, and machine learning model updating for continuous fiber additive manufacturing.

High-Rate Digital Twins

Fast model assimilation, microsecond-latency finite element updates, and time-critical structural decision support.

Smart Sensing Networks

Flexible sensor arrays and wireless sensing for fatigue crack monitoring, concrete structures, and cyber-physical infrastructure systems.
